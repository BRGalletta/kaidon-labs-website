Create a modern, professional website for an AI solutions agency. The site should be clean, conversion-focused, and establish credibility in the AI space.

## Required Pages & Sections

### 1. Landing Page (Hero Section)

* Company Name: Kaidon Labs

* One-liner tagline: Practical AI, built around how your business actually works.

* Primary CTA: Prominent "Book a Meeting" button that links to: https://calendly.com/brian-kaidonlabs/30min

* Design: Clean, modern layout with professional typography

* Hero image/visual: Consider AI-themed graphics or abstract tech visuals

### 2. Services Section

Create a clean services area highlighting:

* Custom AI Solutions: Tailored AI implementations for specific business needs

* AI Chatbots: Intelligent conversational agents for customer service and support

* AI Consulting: Strategic guidance on AI adoption and implementation

* AI Integration: Seamlessly connecting AI tools with existing business systems

### 3. Social Proof Section (Lean Approach)

Since we're keeping social proof minimal for now:

* Simple section titled "What We Do" or "Our Expertise"

* Brief, professional copy about your AI specializations

* Focus on capabilities rather than client testimonials

* Consider including: "Trusted by businesses looking to leverage AI for competitive advantage"

## Design Requirements

- Style: Modern, professional, tech-forward

- Color scheme: Professional palette (blues, grays, whites with accent colors)

- Typography: Clean, readable fonts

- Mobile responsive: Must work perfectly on all devices

- Fast loading: Optimized for speed and performance

## Key Features

- Calendly integration for meeting booking

- Contact form as secondary CTA

- Professional email address display

- Social media links (if applicable)

- Simple navigation menu

- Footer with contact information

## Content Tone

- Professional but approachable

- Focus on business transformation through AI

- Avoid overly technical jargon

- Emphasize practical, results-driven solutions

## Technical Notes

- Ensure the Calendly link opens in a new tab or embedded iframe

- Include basic SEO optimization

- Ensure GDPR compliance for contact forms

## Call-to-Action Strategy

- Primary CTA: "Book a Meeting" (Calendly link)

- Secondary CTA: "Get in Touch" (contact form)

- Keep CTAs prominent and conversion-focused
