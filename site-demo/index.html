Before You Try It

Questions people usually ask first

Is this actually free?

Yes. There's no charge to run the demo and no obligation afterward. If you want the real thing built for your business, that's a separate conversation with Brian — never something this page pressures you into.

What does Kaidon Labs do with my site's content?

We read your homepage's public content (and a couple of obviously-linked pages) only to power this specific demo session, and store it briefly for that purpose. It's never published, resold, or used for anything beyond showing you this preview.

What if it doesn't work well on my site?

Some sites — especially ones built entirely with JavaScript and little server-rendered content — are harder to read automatically. If that happens, the assistant will say so honestly rather than making things up, and we're glad to talk about what a full, properly-built version would look like instead.

Will someone follow up with me after?

Brian gets notified when you run this demo, and may reach out — but there's no sales pitch baked into the demo itself, and no pressure either way.
